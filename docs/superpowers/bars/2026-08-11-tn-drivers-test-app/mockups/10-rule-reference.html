Rule reference

Railroad crossings

You came here from the explanation on question 8.

The rule

A train cannot stop for you. At every crossing, the whole job of stopping is yours — and you may not enter the tracks until you can get all the way across.

What that requires of you

  • Slow at the round yellow sign. It means a crossing is ahead. Reduce speed, look both ways, listen, and be ready to stop before you reach the rails.
  • Stop 15 to 50 feet from the nearest rail whenever the lights are flashing, a gate is lowering or down, a flagger signals, or a train is close enough to be a hazard.
  • Never drive around a lowered gate. Not to save a minute, not because you can see the track is clear. Wait for the gate to rise and the lights to stop.
  • Never start across without room on the far side. Stopping halfway onto a crossing is how vehicles get struck.
  • Don't shift gears on the crossing. A stall on the rails is the one mistake you cannot undo.
  • More than one track means more than one train. After a train passes, wait until you can see clearly in both directions before you move.
  • Some vehicles stop every time. School buses and vehicles carrying passengers for hire or placarded hazardous materials must stop at crossings whether or not a train is coming.

What the manual says

“When you come to a railroad crossing, look both ways and listen for a train. Never start to cross until you are sure there is room for your vehicle on the other side. Never shift gears while crossing. When the signals are flashing or the gates are down, you must stop not less than fifteen (15) feet and not more than fifty (50) feet from the nearest rail, and you must not proceed until the train has passed and the signals have stopped.”

Tennessee Comprehensive Driver License Manual · Section: Railroad Crossings · p. 41

Signs that carry this rule

Crossing ahead

A warning sign. Posted before the tracks.

 Crossbuck

Regulatory. At the rails, it has the force of a yield sign.

 Stop

Some crossings add one. Then a full stop is required every time.
