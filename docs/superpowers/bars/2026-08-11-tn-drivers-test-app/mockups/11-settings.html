Where these questions come from

One source, named on every question, with its page.

Primary source

Tennessee Comprehensive Driver License Manual

Published by the Tennessee Department of Safety & Homeland Security. 135 pages. Its own front matter states the content is current as of 1 July 2022. The same PDF also carries a March 2025 printing authorization; the department does not reconcile the two dates, so we record both and treat July 2022 as the content date.

Questions are drawn from Sections B and C only, in the proportions the manual publishes for the test itself: a quarter each on traffic signs and signals, safe driving principles, rules of the road, and drugs and alcohol. The manual states it is not copyrighted and may be reproduced.

TN Drive is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the State of Tennessee or its Department of Safety & Homeland Security.

Nothing here is an official score, a permit, or a substitute for the manual.

What we've corrected since 2022

The manual disclaims its own currency. Where Tennessee law has moved since July 2022 we teach the current rule — and we say so, here and on every question that touches it. Three corrections are in force today.

Corrected In force 1 Jan 2023

Minimum liability insurance

Manual
$25,000 / $50,000 / $15,000  · p. 18
We teach
$25,000 / $50,000 / $25,000

Bodily injury minimums are unchanged — $25,000 for one person, $50,000 for one crash. The property-damage minimum rose from $15,000 to $25,000. Answer a question on this and you'll see the same note under the rule. Verified against Tennessee's financial-responsibility minimums · checked 1 July 2026

Corrected In force 2023

Move Over Law — who you must move over for

Manual
Emergency, utility and highway-maintenance vehicles  · p. 68
We teach
Any stopped vehicle showing hazard lights, warning devices or flares — including an ordinary car on the shoulder

Tennessee widened the law in 2023. The duty is the same as it always was — move over a lane if you safely can, otherwise slow down — but it now covers far more vehicles than the 2022 manual lists. Verified against the amended Move Over statute · checked 1 July 2026

Corrected In force since 2022 amendment

Aggravated vehicular homicide — the BAC threshold

Manual
BAC 0.20  · p. 96
We teach
BAC 0.15

The threshold that lifts vehicular homicide to the aggravated offense is 0.15, not the 0.20 printed in the manual. The 0.08 limit for driving under the influence, and 0.02 for drivers under 21, are unchanged. Verified against the Tennessee Code · checked 1 July 2026

We never change an answer quietly. If a question's answer differs from the printed manual, the difference is shown on the question, with the manual's wording next to ours.
