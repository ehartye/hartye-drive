> Source: [tn.gov DL_Manual.pdf](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/safety/documents/DL_Manual.pdf).

| # | Claim | Value | Source | Conf. |

| 1.2 | Passing score | **80% minimum** (24 of 30 correct) | same as above | High (PRIMARY) |
| 1.3 | Time limit | **60 minutes** | same as above | High (PRIMARY) |

**1.5 — Early termination after N wrong answers: YES.**
> "Once seven (7) questions are missed the test will automatically shut off. This is the same
> procedure whether in a driver service center, and at home."

Source: `https://safetysupport.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/25774731803283-At-Home-Knowledge-Testing`
(official TN Dept. of Safety help center). Confidence: **Medium-high** — PRIMARY domain, but retrieved
via search-engine cache because the live page redirects to a sign-in wall. The rule is
mathematically consistent with 1.1/1.2: after 7 misses the best possible score is 23/30 = 76.7%,
below the 80% threshold, so the test cannot be passed.

**1.6 — Per-question time limit (at-home only): 2 minutes.**
> "If taking the test at home and you stay on one (1) question over two (2) minutes, the test will
> shut off."
> "If you are taking the test at home, and if you reach the thirty (30) minute mark the proctor has
> to verify that the child is still taking the test, otherwise the test will cut off."
> "All of these scenarios lead to automatic failure."

Same source and same confidence caveat as 1.5.

**1.7 — Retake / waiting period.**
Online (at-home) test: **must wait 24 hours before retesting**, and **a maximum of two (2) online
attempts** are allowed. After two failures the applicant must test in person at a Driver Services
Center.
Source: [tn.gov knowledge-test-online](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl/knowledge-test-online.html) — PRIMARY, **high** confidence.
The in-person retake waiting period is **not published** on tn.gov — see Open questions §9.1.

**1.8 — Cost of the knowledge test.**
No standalone knowledge-test fee appears anywhere in the official fee schedule
([tn.gov Driver License Fees](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/helpful-information/fees.html)).
The test cost is subsumed in the permit/license fee. Confidence: **Medium** — this is an absence of
evidence in an authoritative fee table, not an affirmative statement that the test is free.

**1.9 — Online vs. in-person.**
The online/at-home knowledge test is restricted to applicants **"at least 15 years old and no older
than 17 years old."** Adults (18+) must test at a Driver Services Center.
Sources: [tn.gov knowledge-test-online](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl/knowledge-test-online.html);
[TN Dept. of Safety news release, May 16, 2022](https://www.tn.gov/safety/news/2022/5/16/driver-services-offers-at-home-knowledge-testing.html)
("Teenagers between the ages of 15 to 17 may now take their written knowledge test online").
Confidence: **High** (PRIMARY).

**1.10 — Online test proctoring requirements.**
A parent or legal guardian serves as proctor and may proctor **up to five (5) applicants**. Proctor
must install the **Tennessee Proctor ID app**. Mid-test re-authentication occurs at the **30-minute
mark** via QR-code scan. Applicant must use a computer, laptop, or tablet — **cell phones are not
permitted**. Supported browsers: Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox. Leaving the test window causes
automatic failure.
Source: [tn.gov knowledge-test-online](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl/knowledge-test-online.html) — PRIMARY, **high**.

**1.11 — What to bring (adult first-time Class D applicant).**
- One proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence (original documents; **photocopies rejected**)
- One proof of Social Security Number
- **Two** proofs of Tennessee residency (generally dated within the last 4 months)
- All name-change documentation, if applicable
- Must pass a standard vision screening
- Road-skills-test applicants must be accompanied by someone 21+ with a valid license; a learner
  permit holder cannot drive to the test alone

Source: [tn.gov Adult First-Time Drivers](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/new-drivers.html) — PRIMARY, **high**.

**1.12 — Teen learner permit additional documents.**
Adds: certified birth certificate; **Form SF-1010** (Proof of Compulsory School Attendance and
Satisfactory Progress); parent/guardian signature on the Teenage Affidavit of Financial
Responsibility.
Sources: [tn.gov Teen/GDL](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl.html);
[Form SF-1256 PDF](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/safety/documents/gdl50hrcertificate.pdf) — PRIMARY, **high**.

## 2. Road sign portion

**2.1 — Separate sign test? No separate sign test is published by the State.**
Tennessee publishes exactly one Class D knowledge test: 30 questions, 80% to pass. No official
tn.gov page describes a standalone road-sign examination, and no official page states a sign-question
count or a per-topic breakdown. Signs are therefore embedded in the single 30-question test.
Sources: [tn.gov knowledge-test-online](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl/knowledge-test-online.html);
[tn.gov Adult First-Time Drivers](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/new-drivers.html)
(lists only "the Regular (Class D) Driver License knowledge test" plus a vision screening and a road
skills test).
Confidence: **Medium** — this is a well-supported negative finding (no official source anywhere
describes a separate sign test), but the State has not affirmatively published a topic breakdown.

**2.2 — Number of sign questions: NOT PUBLISHED.** See Open questions §9.2. Commercial test-prep
sites assert a four-section split (road signs and signals / road rules / drugs and alcohol / safe
driving practices), but this appears in **no** official source and should not be treated as fact.

## 3. Graduated Driver License (GDL) program

Primary sources throughout: [tn.gov Teen/Graduated Driver License](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl.html)
and official form [SF-1256, "Certification of 50 Hours Behind the Wheel Driving Experience"](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/safety/documents/gdl50hrcertificate.pdf).
Statutory basis: Tenn. Code Ann. § 55-50-311.

### 3.1 Learner Permit (Class PD)

| Rule | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum age | **15** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Minimum holding period before advancing | **180 days** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Supervision | Licensed driver **21 or older, riding in the front seat** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Nighttime prohibition | **10:00 p.m. – 6:00 a.m.** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Seat belts | Mandatory for all passengers ages 4–17; child restraint if under 4 | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| Fee | **$10.50** (under 18) / **$5.50** (18+) | High (PRIMARY, fee schedule) |

### 3.2 Supervised driving hours

**50 hours total, including a minimum of 10 hours at night**, certified on **Form SF-1256** and signed
by a parent, legal guardian, or licensed driving instructor.
Source: [SF-1256 PDF](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/safety/documents/gdl50hrcertificate.pdf) (form
title verbatim: "CERTIFICATION OF 50 HOURS BEHIND THE WHEEL DRIVING EXPERIENCE"); corroborated by the
[Teen/GDL page](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl.html) ("50 hours of behind
the wheel driving experience including 10 hours of night driving"). Confidence: **High** (PRIMARY).
Note: the SF-1256 PDF carries a print revision stamp of "SF-1256 (02/13)"; its content matches the
current tn.gov HTML pages, so it is treated as current.

### 3.3 Intermediate Restricted License

| Rule | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum age | **16** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Prerequisite | Held learner permit **180 days**; 50/10 hours certified | High (PRIMARY) |
| Driving-record bar | Cannot have **6 or more points** in the 180 days immediately preceding application | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| Passenger limit | **No more than one (1) passenger** | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| **Nighttime prohibition** | **11:00 p.m. – 6:00 a.m.** | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| Seat belts | Mandatory for all passengers ages 4–17 | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |

**Passenger-limit exemptions** (per Tenn. Code Ann. § 55-50-311): the one-passenger cap does not apply
where (a) one or more passengers is 21+ and holds a valid unrestricted license, or (b) the additional
passengers are the driver's siblings/stepsiblings (including adopted or foster children in the same
household) **and** the driver carries a parent/guardian letter authorizing them, **for the sole
purpose of travel to and from school**.
Confidence: **Medium** — SECONDARY (FindLaw restatement of the statute; Justia returned HTTP 403 and
tn.gov does not spell out the exemptions). The one-passenger number itself is High/PRIMARY.

**Nighttime-curfew exemptions** (per § 55-50-311, SECONDARY paraphrase): accompanied by a
parent/guardian or a licensed driver 21+ designated by the parent/guardian; travel to/from
school-sponsored activities with parental permission; travel to/from employment with parental
permission; hunting or fishing between 4:00 and 6:00 a.m. with a valid license.
Confidence: **Medium-low** — SECONDARY paraphrase only. Verify exact wording before teaching it.

### 3.4 Intermediate Unrestricted License

| Rule | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum age | **17** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Prerequisite | Held Intermediate Restricted for **1 year** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Record requirements | No more than **6 points**; **no at-fault crash**; **no more than 1 safety-belt violation** | High (PRIMARY) |
| Second seat-belt violation penalty | Adds **90 days** to the Intermediate Restricted period | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| Additional tests | **None required** to advance | High (PRIMARY) |
| Restrictions lifted | Passenger limit and nighttime curfew both lift; seat belts still mandatory for passengers 4–17; card still reads "intermediate" | High (PRIMARY, SF-1256) |
| Fee | **$2.00** | High (PRIMARY, fee schedule) |

### 3.5 Full Class D License

> "18 years of age, or when you graduate from high school or receive a GED, whichever is sooner"

Source: [tn.gov Teen/GDL](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/classd/teengdl.html) — PRIMARY, **high**.
Note this is an important nuance: **high-school graduation or GED accelerates the transition to a full
Class D license before age 18.**

### 3.6 Changes to the GDL structure since July 1, 2022

**No structural change confirmed.** The ages (15/16/17/18), the 180-day and 1-year holding periods,
the 50/10 supervised-hours requirement, the one-passenger limit, and both curfews (10 p.m. and
11 p.m.) are consistent across current tn.gov pages, Form SF-1256, and the current text of
§ 55-50-311. Confidence: **Medium-high** (a confirmed-consistent finding, not a line-by-line statutory
diff — Justia's TN Code pages returned 403 throughout).

**One adjacent 2025 change does exist** — see §5.8 (bullying-related license suspension for minors).

## 5. Laws that changed after July 1, 2022

### 5.1 Move Over Law — EXPANDED, effective July 1, 2023 ⚠️ MAJOR

**The "Jabari Bailey Highway Safety Act"** (HB0092 / Public Chapter 354, 113th General Assembly) now
> "requires drivers to change lanes or reduce speed upon approaching a stationary vehicle that has
> flashing hazard lights activated; increases fines for failing to change lanes or reduce speed upon
> approaching a stationary emergency, recovery, highway maintenance, solid waste, utility service, or
> other vehicle that has flashing lights activated."

Source: [wapp.capitol.tn.gov HB0092 (113th GA)](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Billinfo/default.aspx?BillNumber=HB0092&ga=113) — **PRIMARY**, confidence **High**.
Effective date: **July 1, 2023**. Amends TCA Title 55, Chapter 8 (§ 55-8-132).

**What changed in substance:** the duty formerly ran only to an enumerated list of official vehicles
(emergency, recovery/tow, highway maintenance, solid waste, utility service). It now extends to **any
stationary vehicle displaying activated flashing hazard lights** — i.e. an ordinary broken-down
civilian car on the shoulder.

**Penalty tiers** (1st: Class B misdemeanor, $250–$500 and/or up to 30 days; 2nd: Class B misdemeanor,
$1,000; 3rd+: Class A misdemeanor, $2,500): confidence **Medium** — SECONDARY sources; the bill
caption confirms fines were increased but does not itself enumerate the tiers.

**⚠️ TDOT's own page is stale.** [tn.gov/tdot/work-with-us/move-over-law.html](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/work-with-us/move-over-law.html)
still describes only the pre-2023 enumerated-vehicle version and states a "maximum fine of up to $500
and possibly up to 30 days in jail." **Do not source Move Over content from that page.** This was
verified by direct fetch on 2026-08-11.

### 5.2 Minimum auto liability insurance — RAISED, effective January 1, 2023 ⚠️

Current Tennessee minimums, quoted verbatim from the Dept. of Revenue:
> "$25,000 for each injury or death per accident. $50,000 for total injuries or deaths per accident.
> **$25,000 for property damage per accident.**"

Source: [tn.gov Dept. of Revenue — Financial Responsibility Law](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/title-and-registration/drive-insured-tennessee/why-you-should-have-insurance.html) — **PRIMARY**, confidence **High**.

**What changed:** property-damage minimum went from **$15,000 → $25,000** (25/50/15 → **25/50/25**),
effective for proof required after December 31, 2022, with existing policyholders given until their
first renewal after that date. Confidence on the *old* $15,000 figure and the transition mechanism:
**Medium** (SECONDARY, quoting the statutory transition clause of TCA § 55-12-102).

**Verified as of 2026:** the current minimums are still 25/50/25 — the tn.gov Revenue page states these
figures today. TCA § 55-12-102 contains a CPI-indexing clause permitting annual January 1 adjustment
rounded to the nearest $1,000; no adjustment above 25/50/25 has been reflected on the state page.
Confidence **High** on the current numbers; **Low** on whether the CPI mechanism has ever been applied
(see Open questions §9.5).

### 5.3 Hands-free law (TCA § 55-8-199) — no substantive change found

The law took effect **July 1, 2019** and remains in force. Prohibits physically holding or supporting
a wireless telecommunications device with any part of the body while driving; also bars texting,
watching video, and recording/broadcasting video. Hands-free/Bluetooth/speakerphone permitted.

Penalties: Class C misdemeanor; fine not to exceed **$50** (plus court costs not to exceed $10);
**$100** if third-or-subsequent offense or if the violation results in an accident; **$200** in a work
zone with workers present or an active school zone. Points are assessed to the driving record.

Sources: [TN Highway Safety Office, Operation Hands Free, April 6, 2026](https://www.tn.gov/safety/news/2026/4/6/tennessee-highway-safety-office-launces-operation-hands-free.html)
(PRIMARY — confirms the law is current and active as of April 2026, and that violations put points on
the record, but does not itself list fine amounts);
[TN General Assembly fiscal note, SB1751/HB2290](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Bills/111/Fiscal/FM1816.pdf) (PRIMARY, penalty structure).
Confidence: **Medium-high** on the prohibition and the $50/$100/$200 tiers; **Medium** on there having
been no amendment since 2022 (no amending Public Chapter was located, but no exhaustive statutory diff
was performed).

### 5.4 Aggravated vehicular assault / homicide — BAC threshold LOWERED, effective July 1, 2025 ⚠️

The BAC threshold that elevates an offense to aggravated vehicular assault / aggravated vehicular
homicide was lowered from **0.20% → 0.15%**.
Bill: HB0190 / SB0457, **Public Chapter 430**, 114th General Assembly. Signed May 9, 2025 (House 93-0,
Senate 28-0). Amends TCA §§ 39-13-115 and 39-13-218 (Title 39 criminal offenses, not the Title 55 DUI
chapter).
Source: [wapp.capitol.tn.gov HB0190 (114th GA)](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Billinfo/default.aspx?BillNumber=HB0190&ga=114) — **PRIMARY**, confidence **High**.

### 5.5 Baseline BAC limits — unchanged

Standard driver **0.08%**; commercial driver **0.04%**; under-21 zero-tolerance **0.02%**.
No change found since 2022. Source: TCA § 55-10-401 (via FindLaw restatement, updated Jan 2, 2024).
Confidence: **Medium** — SECONDARY; the 0.08% and 0.04% figures are federally standard and
uncontroversial, the 0.02% under-21 figure was not independently confirmed against primary text.

### 5.6 Ignition interlock — GPS geotagging required, effective January 1, 2024

Ignition interlock devices installed on or after **January 1, 2024** must have GPS "geotagging"
capability (event-triggered on startup test, random retest, skipped test, or circumvention detection —
not continuous tracking). Amends TCA § 55-10-411.
Bill: HB0077 / SB0258, **Public Chapter 20**, 113th General Assembly. Signed March 6, 2023.
Source: [wapp.capitol.tn.gov HB0077 (113th GA)](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Billinfo/default.aspx?BillNumber=HB0077&ga=113) — **PRIMARY**, confidence **High**.
*Relevance to a knowledge test: low. Included for completeness.*

### 5.7 Seat belt and child restraint law — no change found

Current thresholds (TCA §§ 55-9-602, 55-9-603):

- Under 1 year **or** 20 lbs or less: rear-facing restraint, rear seat if available
- 1–3 years **and** over 20 lbs: forward-facing restraint, rear seat if available
- 4–8 years **and** under 4'9": belt-positioning booster seat
- 9–12 years, or any child through 12 measuring 4'9" or taller: seat belt system
- Age 16+: seat belt required; **primary enforcement**
- Penalty: Class C misdemeanor, $50 fine, no court costs

Source: FindLaw restatement of TCA § 55-9-602, cross-checked against 2020/2021/2024 code versions —
**SECONDARY**, confidence **Medium**. No amendment enacted 2023–2026 was found, but Justia's primary
code pages returned HTTP 403 so no line-by-line diff was possible.

### 5.8 Minor's license suspension for bullying/cyberbullying — NEW, effective July 1, 2025

Tennessee courts may suspend a minor's driver license for **up to one year** following a juvenile-court
bullying or cyberbullying adjudication. First-time offenders may apply within **10 days** for a
restricted license (school/work commuting only — no extracurriculars or social events) for a **$20**
fee.
Sources: SECONDARY only — multiple independent national outlets (CNN, Fox News, ABC News, WSMV, WVLT),
July 2025. Confidence: **Medium** — broad independent corroboration, but the bill/Public Chapter text
was not retrieved.

## 6. REAL ID — status as of 2026-08-11

**6.1 — REAL ID enforcement is IN EFFECT. The May 7, 2025 date has passed.**
> "REAL ID went into effect on May 7, 2025."

A REAL ID-compliant credential (or passport, military ID, or other TSA-acceptable ID) is required to
board commercial domestic flights, enter certain federal facilities, and access nuclear power plants.
REAL ID is **optional** for ordinary purposes — driving, buying alcohol, etc.
Source: [tn.gov REAL ID](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/helpful-information/real-id.html) — PRIMARY, **high**.
Corroborated by [DHS, "TSA Begins REAL ID Full Enforcement," May 7, 2025](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/05/07/tsa-begins-real-id-full-enforcement).

**6.2 — Application must be in person.**
> "Federal law requires REAL ID applications to be made in person."

At a Driver Services Center or a participating County Clerk. If you have no existing TN license/ID, you
must go to a **full-service** Driver Services Center. Documents can be pre-approved at
**tnrealid.gov** to avoid repeat trips.
Source: same tn.gov REAL ID page — PRIMARY, **high**.

**6.3 — Documents required.** One proof of U.S. citizenship or legal presence; one proof of Social
Security number; two proofs of Tennessee residency; documentation of any name changes.
Source: same — PRIMARY, **high**.

**6.4 — Cost.** No surcharge; same as a standard TN license or photo ID. See §4.

**6.5 — NEW as of February 1, 2026: TSA ConfirmID, $45 fee.** ⚠️
Travelers who do not present an acceptable form of ID may pay a **$45 fee** to use **TSA ConfirmID**, a
modernized alternative identity-verification system, to establish identity at the checkpoint. Coverage
is valid for a **10-day travel period**; travel outside that window requires paying again. Users are
subject to additional screening and delays of **up to 30 minutes**. Fee is paid in advance at
`tsa.gov/ConfirmID` via pay.gov.
Sources: [TSA press release, Dec 1, 2025](https://www.tsa.gov/news/press/releases/2025/12/01/tsa-introduces-new-45-fee-option-for-travelers-without-real-id);
[TSA press release, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.tsa.gov/news/press/releases/2026/01/15/45-fee-option-for-air-travelers-without-real-id-begins-february-1).
Confidence: **High** — PRIMARY domain (tsa.gov); the release pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch,
so text was obtained via search-engine cache of those tsa.gov URLs, corroborated by
[DoD Defense Travel Management Office](https://www.travel.dod.mil/About/News/Article/Article/4393216/travelers-without-real-id-could-pay-45-fee-for-tsas-confirmid-beginning-februar/) (also .mil/PRIMARY).

**6.6 — Phased enforcement.** TSA's January 2025 final rule permits federal agencies to phase in REAL
ID enforcement based on security, operational feasibility, and public impact.
Source: [TSA final rule press release, Jan 13, 2025](https://www.tsa.gov/news/press/releases/2025/01/13/tsa-publishes-final-rule-real-id-enforcement-beginning-may-7-2025) — PRIMARY, **medium-high**.

## 8. ⚠️ Conflicts with the 2022 manual

This is the operative list. Each item is something the app **must not** teach from the 2022 manual.

| # | Topic | What the 2022-era manual reflects | What is true now | Effective | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **Move Over Law** | Duty runs only to enumerated official vehicles (emergency, recovery/tow, highway maintenance, solid waste, utility service); max fine ~$500 / up to 30 days | Duty extends to **any stationary vehicle with activated flashing hazard lights**, including ordinary disabled civilian cars; fines increased with tiered penalties | **July 1, 2023** (PC 354, Jabari Bailey Highway Safety Act) | **High** (PRIMARY) |
| **C2** | **Minimum liability insurance** | 25/50/**15** — $15,000 property damage | 25/50/**25** — **$25,000** property damage | **Jan 1, 2023** | **High** (PRIMARY) |
| **C3** | **REAL ID** | Frames May 7, 2025 as a **future** deadline | Deadline **passed**; enforcement is live. Additionally, **TSA ConfirmID $45 fee** option exists for travelers without acceptable ID, 10-day validity | May 7, 2025; ConfirmID **Feb 1, 2026** | **High** (PRIMARY) |
| **C4** | **Aggravated vehicular assault / homicide BAC** | Elevating threshold **0.20%** | Elevating threshold **0.15%** | **July 1, 2025** (PC 430) | **High** (PRIMARY) |
| **C5** | **Ignition interlock** | No GPS requirement | Devices installed on/after Jan 1, 2024 must have GPS geotagging capability | **Jan 1, 2024** (PC 20) | **High** (PRIMARY) |
| **C6** | **Knowledge test mechanics** | Manual does not describe early termination | Test **auto-terminates after 7 missed questions**; at-home test also terminates on a single question exceeding **2 minutes** and on failed 30-minute proctor re-auth | Current policy | **Medium-high** (PRIMARY domain, cached) |

## 9. Open questions

Items that could **not** be resolved from an authoritative source. No numbers are asserted for these.

1. **In-person knowledge test retake waiting period.** The 24-hour rule and the 2-attempt cap are
   documented **only** for the online/at-home test. tn.gov publishes no waiting period for retesting
   at a Driver Services Center. Unknown whether one exists.
2. **Number of road-sign questions on the 30-question test**, and whether the State uses a fixed
   topic breakdown at all. No official source publishes this. Commercial prep sites assert a
   four-section split; that assertion has no official backing and should not be relied on.
3. **Whether the knowledge test carries any standalone fee.** Absent from the official fee schedule,
   which implies it is bundled into the permit/license fee, but this is inference, not a published
   statement.
4. ~~Whether the Comprehensive Driver License Manual has been revised since 2022.~~ **RESOLVED — it
   has not.** The PDF served at the official tn.gov URL is still the July 1, 2022 edition, 135 pages,
   carrying the original disclaimer (quoted at the top of this file). An earlier search result
   suggesting a "May 7, 2025" manual effective date appears to have been echoing the REAL ID
   enforcement date, not a manual revision. Confidence **Medium-high** — the PDF exceeds the direct
   fetch size limit, so the disclaimer text came from a search-engine cache of that exact tn.gov URL;
   worth a 30-second local confirmation by opening the PDF. **Every conflict in §8 remains live.**
5. **Whether the TCA § 55-12-102 CPI-indexing clause has ever produced an adjustment above 25/50/25.**
   The statute permits annual January 1 adjustment rounded to the nearest $1,000. tn.gov currently
   states 25/50/25, so either no adjustment has occurred or the page is not tracking it.
6. **Public Chapter numbers and exact text** for: the slowpoke-law fine increase, the LESTER Act, the
   suspended-license points cap, the roadway-solicitation and impeding-traffic misdemeanors,
   "Ethan, Hailey and Bentley's Law," and the 100%-sentence rule for vehicular homicide by
   intoxication. Next step for any of these: pull the 2025 Public Chapters PDF directly from
   `https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/PublicChapters/` and cross-reference bill
   numbers.
7. **Whether `tn.gov/dlpractice.html` is a live practice-test resource.** The page returned navigation
   chrome only.
8. **Exact statutory wording of the intermediate-restricted passenger and curfew exemptions.** Only a
   secondary paraphrase of TCA § 55-50-311 was obtainable (Justia returned HTTP 403 throughout;
   tn.gov does not publish the exemptions). If the app teaches exemptions, pull the statute text from
   a primary source first.
9. **Whether 2023 Public Chapter 100 (§ 17), which amends TCA Title 55 Chapter 50 effective
   July 1, 2024, touches § 55-50-311 (the GDL section).** Reference surfaced in search but content was
   not retrieved. Should be resolved before treating the GDL rules as fully unchanged.
10. **Whether TCA Title 55 Chapter 30 (Automated Vehicles Act)** was enacted or amended after
    July 1, 2022, or is pre-existing.
11. **Whether TCA § 55-8-199 (hands-free) was amended at all between 2022 and 2026.** No amending
    Public Chapter was found, but no exhaustive statutory diff was performed.
