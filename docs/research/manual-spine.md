  - PDF page 14: Section A divider (unnumbered)

- Quotes are verbatim from the extracted text. Line breaks introduced by PDF extraction have been collapsed to single spaces; nothing else was altered. ## Phrasing style observations (for question-bank generation)

- Almost every question uses exactly **three options (A/B/C)**, never four.
- "Both of the above" / "All of the above" / "A & B" / "Both A and C" appears in **9 of 27** questions (Q1, Q4, Q8, Q10, Q16, Q24, Q27, plus the composite forms). It is the correct answer in most of those cases — a real answer-position bias in the source.
- Questions are stated as sentence stems completed by the option, not as full interrogatives, roughly half the time.
- Numeric questions offer one clearly-too-small, one correct, and one clearly-too-large distractor (Q3, Q13, Q25).
- The manual explicitly warns the real test may contain "completely different questions, in whole or in part."

# 6. Content caveats

Flagged, not fixed. Each item is something that should be resolved against current Tennessee Code before a question is generated from it.

## C1 — The manual's own currency disclaimer

The document states it is current as of **July 1, 2022** (PDF p. 3) and explicitly disclaims itself:

> "Misprints or outdated information that may appear within these pages will not override or supersede changes that have occurred in the law, promulgated rules and regulations or policy that has been initiated since the printing date."

But the back cover (PDF p. 135) stamps the print run as **March 2025**, meaning this physical copy was printed nearly three years after its stated content date. Any rule sourced from this manual is at minimum a 2022-vintage statement of law.

## C2 — REAL ID date has passed

Pages 4–5 (PDF) describe the REAL ID enforcement date as prospective: *"Beginning May 7, 2025, all persons must have a REAL ID license..."* and *"The currently scheduled effective date is May 7, 2025."* That date is in the past relative to any current use of this document. Do not generate questions treating it as a future date.

## C3 — Back-seat safety belt rule has an internal gap at age 17

PDF p. 45 says belts are required for back-seat passengers *"If the passengers are under 17 years old"*, then immediately says *"This provision no longer applies when back seat passengers are 18 years or older."* Those two sentences leave **age 17 undefined** — under 17 is covered, 18+ is exempt, 17 is neither. Do not generate a question about a 17-year-old back-seat passenger from this text.

## C4 — Move Over Law scope is stated two different ways

- PDF p. 69 (Ch. B-4): applies *"only on multi-lane roadways where there are two or more lanes of traffic moving in the same direction."*
- PDF p. 86 (Ch. B-5): applies to *"any multi-lane highway (with four or more lanes) that has at least two lanes of traffic traveling in the same direction."*

The four-lane qualifier appears in one place and not the other. A question asking "on what roads does the Move Over Law apply?" has two defensible answers in this document.

## C5 — License renewal cycle: 5 years vs. 8 years

- PDF p. 25: *"As of January 1, 2016, Tennessee licenses for persons over the age of 21 expire every eight years."*
- PDF p. 26: *"Your Class D license issued before January 1, 2016 normally expires in 5 years on your birthdate."*
- PDF p. 33 (Teen FAQ): *"...until that license is at the end of its five-year renewal cycle."*

The teen-FAQ statement on p. 33 uses the pre-2016 five-year cycle without qualification, contradicting the current eight-year rule.

## C6 — Temporary Driver License fee arithmetic contradicts the fee table

PDF p. 25 text: *"If getting a TDL for a motorcycle the additional $1.00 motorcycle fee will apply for a total of $20.50 for XM"* and *"$38.00 for a Class-Type XDM."* Table 2.4 on PDF p. 26 lists **XM at $29.00** and **XDM at $55.00**, and the base TDL fee is stated as $28.00 on p. 25. The $20.50 and $38.00 figures cannot be reconciled with either the base fee or the table. Do not generate TDL fee questions.

## C7 — GDL fee figures differ between Table 2.4 and Table 3.2

Table 2.4 (PDF p. 26) lists Intermediate Restricted – D at **$23.25**. Table 3.2 (PDF p. 34) lists *"Age 16 = $24.50"* for the same step, and *"Age 17 = $21.00"* which does not correspond to any Table 2.4 line. GDL fee questions are unsafe.

## C8 — Chapter B-3 carries the wrong chapter title

PDF p. 49 heads the traffic-signs chapter as **"Section B-3 GETTING FAMILIAR WITH YOUR VEHICLE"** — a copy-paste of the Chapter B-1 title. The table of contents (PDF p. 10) correctly calls it "Traffic Signs and Signals." Section labels in this manual are not reliable; page numbers are.

## C9 — Internal cross-reference errors

- PDF p. 115 (Chapter C-2) says *"Chapter C-4 of this manual gives you information on sharing the road with various types of traffic"* — sharing the road is **Chapter C-3**, not C-4.
- PDF p. 38 tells parents to *"review Section C-4 starting at page 116"* — that one is correct.
- Chapter C-1's body heading is "DEFENSIVE DRIVING AND OTHER PRECAUTIONS" (PDF p. 107) while the TOC calls it "Defensive Driving and Road Rage."
- Chapter B-8's body heading is "DRIVING RESPONSIBILITIES" (PDF p. 99) while the TOC calls it "Driving Responsibility."

## C10 — Crash-count statistics contradict each other

- PDF p. 6: *"there are approximately 191,065 crashes reported annually to the Department of Safety"*
- PDF p. 112: *"there are approximately 160,000 crashes reported to the Department of Safety annually."*

Do not generate statistics questions from either figure.

## C11 — The right-of-way statement for a dead traffic signal is circular

PDF p. 56: *"if there are two or more vehicles at the intersection stopping at the same time, the driver on the left would yield to the driver on the right. The driver on the right who arrives first gets to go first."* The second sentence conflates "arrived first" with "is on the right" and muddles the rule. The clean statement is on PDF p. 68 (R261) and should be used instead.

## C12 — Bicyclists "may disregard stop signs and traffic signals"

PDF p. 120, under "Residential Areas are Danger Zones for Bicycles": *"Bicyclists may ride in the middle of the street and disregard stop signs and traffic signals."* Read in context this is a warning about what children on bicycles **do**, not a statement of what bicyclists are **permitted** to do — and it directly contradicts PDF p. 119, *"bicyclists have the same rights and responsibilities as other roadway users."* Never generate a question from this sentence.

## C13 — Sample question 25's page pointer is off by one

Question 25 points to "Page 88" for the 20-day reportable-crash filing deadline, but that rule is printed on page **89** (PDF p. 103). Page 88 (PDF p. 102) covers financial responsibility definitions.

## C14 — The texting law and the hands-free law coexist without reconciliation

PDF p. 44 presents both the 2009 texting prohibition (*"Effective July 1, 2009 drivers are prohibited from using a hand-held telephone or hand-held personal digital assistant to transmit or read a written message"*, fine ≤$50 / court costs ≤$10) **and** the 2019 Public Chapter 412 hands-free law, on the same page, without explaining which controls. The 2009 penalty figures may well be superseded. Prefer the hands-free-law content (R377) for question generation and treat the $50/$10 figures as uncertain.

## C15 — Learner permit period stated as both "180 days" and "six months"

PDF p. 28 and the checklist on PDF p. 21 say **180 days**; the Teen Driver FAQ on PDF p. 33 and Table 3.2 on PDF p. 34 say **six months** / *"Held a valid PD for 6 months."* These are not identical periods. Use "180 days" as the primary framing.

## C16 — Section page ranges in the introduction do not match the content

PDF p. 7 says *"Section C ... consists of pages 91-117"*, but the TOC lists the Graduated Driver License Driving Experience Log at printed page 119 and substantive Chapter C-4 content through printed 118.

## C17 — Dated statistics throughout

Numerous figures are pinned to specific past years and will read as stale: 2019 young-driver fatalities (PDF p. 32), 2016 Tennessee traffic deaths of 1,039 (PDF p. 46), 2015 large-truck fatal crashes of 4,311 (PDF p. 123), 2021 work-zone figures (PDF p. 61), 2020 and 2021 alcohol-related fatality percentages (PDF p. 97), and NHTSA air-bag lives-saved through 2012 (PDF p. 47). None of these are rules; avoid them as question fodder.

## C18 — Duplicated / garbled block in the work-zone section

PDF p. 61 contains a fragment *"were 3,010 cases of property damage in work zones crashes in Tennessee."* appearing twice, once orphaned mid-page next to a truncated *"ED / LIMIT POSTED IN AC"* string. This is a layout artifact of the two-column page. `[extraction unclear]`

## C19 — Ignition interlock paragraph begins mid-sentence

PDF p. 96: *"Only a motor vehicle equipped with a functioning ignition interlock device (which keeps a car from starting if the driver's BAC is too high). The restriction can be from six months to one year..."* The first sentence has no verb — the phrase describing what the offender may operate is missing from the extraction and possibly from the original layout. `[extraction unclear]`

## C20 — Yield sign description conflates yielding with stopping

PDF p. 50: *"This three-sided sign means that you are approaching an intersection and must stop and wait when other vehicles are approaching from the right or left."* Sample question 7's keyed answer is "Slow down and yield to traffic on main road when merging" (A), which is the standard meaning. The prose overstates the stop obligation relative to the keyed answer. Use option A's framing.

## C21 — Notable topics the manual does *not* cover

Confirmed absent from the full text — do not invent rules for these:

- **No minimum liability insurance dollar amounts.** The Financial Responsibility section (PDF pp. 102–103) lists acceptable *evidence* of financial responsibility but never states coverage minimums (no $25,000/$50,000/$15,000 figures anywhere in the document).
- **No motorcycle helmet law statement.** The manual says "ALWAYS WEAR A HELMET" as advice (PDF pp. 120–121) and references DOT standards, but never states Tennessee's helmet requirement as law. It defers to the separate *Tennessee Motorcycle Operator Manual*.
- **No bicycle helmet law statement** (advice only, PDF p. 120).
- **No open container law.**
- **No specific window-tint rules for the rear window of a passenger car** beyond the 35% figure and the multi-purpose-vehicle exemption (PDF p. 37).
- **No child restraint rule for children 9–15 by height** beyond the general "driver responsible up to 16" statement.
